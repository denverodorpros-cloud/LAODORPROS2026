The treatment process

Start with the source.
Make a plan for the whole space.

Ozone treatment is one part of addressing an odor problem. We discuss the source, the preparation needed, and what to expect before scheduling.

01

Inspect

Identify the odor source, affected materials, and access needs.

02

Prepare

Remove or clean source material so treatment can reach the affected areas.

03

Treat

Use ozone equipment in a sealed, unoccupied space.

04

Finish

Discuss any appropriate finishing steps, including an optional light scent.

05

Ventilate

Air out the space and follow the technician’s re-entry instructions.
